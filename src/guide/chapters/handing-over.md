# Handing over control

Every record is controlled by one or more teams, which decide who sees it and who changes it. Control moves between
teams only with both sides' agreement: one offers, the other accepts. This chapter shares control of three breeding
lines with the Cooperative, which is to multiply their seed.

> [!WHO]
> To offer: the admins of the teams giving up control. To accept: the admins of the team offered it. Here, Bridget
> Breeder, an admin of the Wheat Breeding Group, then Petra Partner, an admin of the Cooperative.

## Before you start

The other side's team needs to be known to you: the root team of another organisation's team tree is, to everyone
signed in, and so are your own teams.

![Handing over control](handing-over.mp4)

## Steps

1. **Bulk control lists what a team controls.** Bridget chooses the Wheat Breeding Group, then its germplasm, and the
   three lines to share: EPB 21-014, EPB 21-022 and EPB 21-038. {#choose}
2. **Offer transfer… asks the other team to take control.** Bridget offers the lines to the Cooperative, with the
   Wheat Breeding Group keeping its control, so that control is shared. Nothing changes until the offer is accepted.
   {#offer}
3. **The other team's admin accepts.** Petra finds the offer under Transfer offers, with the lines it offers, and
   chooses the Cooperative's team that takes control and the release of its control: Registered. {#accept}
4. **Both teams now control the lines.** A line's controller lists the Cooperative beside the team that controlled it
   already, which Petra sees as a team of the Institute she can't see, and its history shows her acceptance. Either team
   can later renounce its control, as long as one remains. {#shared}

## Who sees what

> [!SEES]
> An offer is seen by the admins of the teams on both sides, under Transfer offers, until it is accepted, rejected or
> cancelled; it is kept as a record after. Once control is shared, those with access to either team see and change the
> lines as their access allows.

> [!NOTE]
> Control is given record by record: sharing a programme does not share its trials and studies, which keep their own
> control teams. To move several records together, choose them all for one offer.
