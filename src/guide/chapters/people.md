# People and credit

Person records credit the people who did the work: as contributors to programmes, trials and datasets, with the roles
they played, and as contacts. A person record is made only with the person's agreement, and is separate from any
account: someone without an account can be credited, and someone with one can link it.

> [!WHO]
> Anyone who can create records can add a person record, and those who curate a programme, trial or dataset credit
> people on it. A request to link an account to a record is decided by the administrators of the team that controls
> the record.

## Steps

1. **+ Add Person records someone to credit.** On People, Bridget adds Hannah Harvester, with her institution and team.
   She confirms that Hannah agreed to be recorded, having been told about the record and the privacy notice, and
   releases the record to registered users, the widest a person record can be. {#add}
2. **People are credited with the roles they played.** Bridget edits the Winter Wheat Breeding Programme and credits
   Hannah as a contributor, in the CRediT role Investigation. {#credit}
3. **Linking an account is approved by the record's team.** Bridget confirms that the request is Petra's, then
   approves it. {#link}
4. **Others see as much as each record is released to them.** Signed in, Petra sees Bridget and Hannah by name and
   institution. Fergus Fieldhand's record is private to his team, so where he is credited, on the 2024 datasets, she
   sees only an id. {#partner}
5. **Contact sends a message without showing the contact's address.** Petra writes to the programme's contact, and
   BreedGraph emails it to Bridget, with Petra's name and address so that Bridget can reply. {#message}
6. **Visitors see no people.** Signed out, the programme shows no contacts or contributors. {#visitor}

## Who sees what

> [!SEES]
> A person record in full, its teams included, is for the teams that control it. Released to registered users, its
> name, institution and ORCID iD are shown to signed-in users wherever the person is credited; others see an id.
> Visitors who are not signed in see no people at all.

> [!DATA]
> A person record holds a name, an institution, teams and, if its person connects one, an ORCID iD: no contact details.
> It is made only once the person has agreed, having been told about the record and the privacy notice, which the form
> asks you to confirm. Once the record is linked to their account, they can correct or erase it; without an account,
> they can ask the University to.
