# Setting up a trial and study

A programme's trials and their studies hold its data. A trial is a stage of the programme, such as a season's yield
trial; a study is one experiment in it, and the datasets recorded in it are its data. This chapter sets up this
season's trial, and the study its plots are recorded in.

> [!WHO]
> To create a trial, those who curate the programme; to create a study, those who curate the trial. Either way, you
> also need Write access to a team, to control what you create.

## Before you start

The programme needs to exist: here, the Winter Wheat Breeding Programme, which Bridget Breeder curates through the
Wheat Breeding Group. The 2026 plots are already sown in Long Meadow, as [Planting a trial](/guide/planting) shows.

![Setting up a trial and study](setting-up.mp4)

## Steps

1. **Create Trial adds a trial to a programme.** On the programme's card, Bridget creates Advanced Yield Trial 2026,
   from October 2025 to August 2026. A date can be partial, and an end covers the whole of its period. She adds
   herself as the trial's contact. {#trial}
2. **Create Study adds a study to the trial.** Long Meadow Yield Trial 2026, with what it compares, how it was sown
   and treated, its dates, and its design: a randomised complete block. {#study}
3. **The control team and release decide who sees and changes it.** The Wheat Breeding Group controls the study, and
   it is released Private: until it is shared, only the teams with access see it, and those who curate it change it.
   {#control}
4. **The study is ready for its data.** Its card shows what was entered, and the trial's shows its contact. The plots'
   observations are recorded into it next, in [Recording observations](/guide/recording). {#ready}

## Who sees what

> [!SEES]
> A private trial or study is seen by the teams with Read access to its control team, directly or as heritable from
> a team above it. Its contacts are shown to those who can see it and are signed in.

> [!DATA]
> A contact is a person record linked to an account, released to registered users. Others can message a contact
> through BreedGraph, which does not show them the contact's address, as in [People and credit](/guide/people).
