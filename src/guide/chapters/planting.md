# Planting a trial

A unit is anything observed: a field's trial area, a plot, a plant, a sample. Units form graphs, from the trial area
down to its plots, and each plot down to the plants and samples taken from it, so that where a unit is, what it is of
and how it was treated pass down the graph to the units below. This chapter plants next season's trial in Hill Field:
its trial area, its plots, and where each plot is in the trial's layout.

> [!WHO]
> Those with Write access to a team, which controls the units they add; to change a unit, those who curate it. Here,
> Theo Technician, of Field Operations.

## Before you start

The field and its layout need to exist, as [Sites, fields and layouts](/guide/places) made them, and the plant
material, as in [Plant material](/guide/plant-material). The subjects (Field, Plot, Plant, ...) are the ontology's, as
in [Describing what you measure](/guide/describing).

## Steps

1. **+ New unit graph starts a graph at a location.** At Hill Field, Theo adds Hill Field 2027 trial area, whose subject
   is Field, placed in Hill Field from the day it was sown. {#graph}
2. **Add children… adds the same children to each unit chosen.** Eighteen plots of the trial area, named by number,
   HF27-1 to HF27-18. With its date emptied, they stay where the trial area is, until each is placed. {#plots}
3. **Each plot is of its own entry.** Edit sets a plot's germplasm, here HF27-1's. The plants and samples later taken
   from it take its germplasm, unless they name their own. {#germplasm}
4. **Add Position places a plot in the layout.** HF27-1 is in Block 1, at row 1 and column 1, from the day it was sown.
   The other plots are given their entries and places the same way. {#position}
5. **The trial is planted.** The unit graph lists the plots under the trial area, ready for their observations, as in
   [Recording observations](/guide/recording). {#planted}

## Who sees what

> [!SEES]
> Units have their own control team and release, as the trial area and plots here are Field Operations' and private.
> A hidden unit is left out of the graph, and the units below it are joined to the nearest unit above that can be seen;
> a hidden root is kept as a placeholder, so the graph still has one.

> [!NOTE]
> A unit's positions are its history: it can move, as samples go from a field to a lab, and each position keeps when it
> started and ended. Move… and End… record those, as in [Samples in the lab](/guide/samples).
