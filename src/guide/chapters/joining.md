# Joining BreedGraph

Accounts are by invitation. Anyone with an account can invite a new address, and someone who administers a team can
offer the newcomer access to it, and a person record that credits them. The newcomer registers from the emailed link,
then verifies their address.

> [!WHO]
> To invite, anyone signed in; to offer a team's access, an administrator of that team. To join, anyone invited.

## Before you start

You need an account to invite someone: here, Bridget Breeder, who administers the Wheat Breeding Group. To join, you
need only the invitation email.

![Joining BreedGraph](joining.mp4)

## Steps

1. **An invitation is sent from your profile.** Bridget opens Profile, then Invite user, and enters Nina's address.
   She offers Read and Write access to the Wheat Breeding Group, and the person record Nina Newcomer, then sends the
   invitation. It is listed as pending until it is used. {#invite}
2. **The invitation arrives by email.** Its link is for registering, once only and for a limited time. {#email}
3. **Nina registers from the link.** She chooses a username and a password; her address is the one invited. She
   accepts the team access offered, links the person record, and consents to the processing of her personal data as
   the privacy notice describes. {#register}
4. **A second email verifies her address.** Its link confirms the address, after which she can sign in. {#verify}
5. **Her profile shows what is held about her.** Her account, the person record linked to it, and her teams.
   {#profile}

## Who sees what

> [!SEES]
> A pending invitation is listed for the person who sent it, who can resend or cancel it. Nina's person record is
> shown only to signed-in users it is released to: a person record is never public.

> [!DATA]
> Registering collects a username, a full name, an email address and a password, which is stored only as a hash. The
> consent is recorded with its time and the version of the privacy notice it was given to. Each can be changed, and
> the account deleted, as [Your account and personal data](/guide/your-data) shows.

## Try it on the demo site

Sign in with a reviewer account and invite your own address. The account you register is removed at the next reset.
