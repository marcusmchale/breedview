# Samples in the lab

Samples are units too. A grain sample taken from a plot stays below the plot in its unit graph, so its results describe
the plot's entry and treatment, wherever the sample goes. This chapter follows the 2025 trial's grain into the
laboratory: subsamples, a germination check, the freezer, and a sample used up.

> [!WHO]
> Those with Write access to a team add units and derive them; moving, ending or correcting units needs curate access to
> them. Here, Lena Lab, of the Grain Quality Lab.

## Before you start

The samples need to have arrived: here, the grain harvested from each 2025 plot, which came to the Grain Quality
Laboratory together. The plots are a unit graph of their own, as in [Planting a trial](/guide/planting).

![Samples in the lab](samples.mp4)

## Steps

1. **Arrivals list what came to a location together.** At the Grain Quality Laboratory, the 48 grain samples that
   arrived on 21 August 2025, each linked to the plot it came from. {#arrival}
2. **Add children… takes a subsample of each.** A flour sample of each grain sample, milled on 2 September: each is
   placed where its grain was then, the laboratory, and takes its grain's germplasm. {#children}
3. **Derive… starts new units from the material.** For a germination check, seed of each sample is sown on the
   Glasshouse benches: 48 seedlings, each a unit of its own that takes its sample's germplasm. The samples stay as they
   were. {#derive}
4. **Move on… sends units elsewhere.** The grain samples go to the Sample Freezer, in a box on its first shelf, from 10
   September. They stay below their plots, and each keeps the history of where it has been. {#move}
5. **End… records a unit that is gone.** One flour sample was used up in the protein analysis: it ends at the
   laboratory, and its record stays. {#end}

## Who sees what

> [!SEES]
> Units are seen as their control teams release them, and the data recorded of them as its datasets are released.

> [!NOTE]
> Keep what is taken for analysis as children, so that its results describe the unit it came from; derive new units
> when the material starts a life of its own, such as seedlings grown from seed.
