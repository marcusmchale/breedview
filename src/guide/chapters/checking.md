# Checking and correcting data

View & curate datasets shows a study's records as a table, as in [Exploring a study's data](/guide/exploring-data).
Those who curate a dataset can also change it there: correct a value, or delete a record. This chapter finds a typo
in the heights just recorded, and corrects it.

> [!WHO]
> Anyone who can see a study's data can view it. To change a dataset's records, you need Curate access to the team
> that controls the dataset.

## Before you start

The data needs to be recorded, as in [Recording observations](/guide/recording). Theo Technician curates Field
Operations' datasets, so he checks the heights he submitted.

![Checking and correcting data](checking.mp4)

## Steps

1. **View & curate datasets opens the study's records.** Theo chooses Plant height (cm), then Load Table. {#open}
2. **Sorting a column brings an outlier to the top.** Plant height, sorted from the highest, starts with LM26-207 at
   830 cm: a typo for 83. {#sort}
3. **A value is corrected where it stands, then saved.** Theo types 83 over it, then Save Changes: ✓ Saved at the
   time. {#correct}
4. **A record's 🔒 shows who controls its dataset.** Field Operations controls the heights, released Private, and the
   write history lists Theo's submission and his correction. {#controller}
5. **Export CSV saves the records to a file.** The corrected heights, in the columns shown, ready for a spreadsheet.
   {#export}

## Who sees what

> [!SEES]
> A correction is seen at once by everyone who can read the dataset. Those who do not curate it see the values, but
> cannot change them.

> [!DATA]
> A dataset's write history records who changed it, and when. It is shown with the dataset's controller, to those who
> can read the dataset.
