# Plant material

Germplasm is the plant material a programme works with: crops, varieties, breeding lines and the crosses between them.
Each entry records where it came from, so the germplasm graph is the programme's pedigree. This chapter records a new
cross, made for next season, and follows a parent back to the plant it was selected from.

> [!WHO]
> Those with Write access to a team, which controls the entry. Here, Bridget Breeder, of the Wheat Breeding Group.

## Before you start

The parents need to be recorded: here, the breeding lines EPB 25-S03 and EPB 21-022. How an entry was produced is
described by the ontology's control methods, such as Hand crossing, as in
[Describing what you measure](/guide/describing).

![Plant material](plant-material.mp4)

## Steps

1. **Create New Entry records a new line.** Bridget names the cross EPB 26-X01, says when and how it was made, sexually,
   by hand crossing, and adds its sources: EPB 25-S03 as its maternal parent and EPB 21-022 as its paternal one. The
   Wheat Breeding Group controls it, and it is private until the line is released. {#entry}
2. **The pedigree graph finds it among the programme's lines.** Found by its name, the new cross is drawn linked to its
   parents, and its card lists them as its sources: EPB 25-S03, maternal, and EPB 21-022, paternal. {#graph}
3. **An entry can name the unit it came from.** From the cross's card, EPB 25-S03 opens its own: it was selected from a
   plant in the 2025 trial, and its card links to that plant on the Units page, with the plot it grew in.
   {#selection}

## Who sees what

> [!SEES]
> A private entry is seen by the teams with Read access to its control team. For anyone else it is left out of the
> pedigree, and the lines bred from it are linked to its nearest ancestors they can see, by a link of unknown kind.

> [!NOTE]
> A unit is of a germplasm entry, and the units below it take that entry unless they name their own, as the plots in
> [Planting a trial](/guide/planting) do.
