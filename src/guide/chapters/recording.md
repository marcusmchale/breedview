# Recording observations

Observations are recorded in a study as datasets, one for each variable or factor: the plots' heights, their disease
scores, the nitrogen each plot received. Submit data makes them from a table of the units observed, typed in or pasted
from a field sheet.

> [!WHO]
> Anyone with Write access to a team can submit data to a study they can see. The team they choose controls the
> datasets.

## Before you start

The study needs to exist, as in [Setting up a trial and study](/guide/setting-up), and so do the units observed: here
the 36 plots of the 2026 trial in Long Meadow, sown as in [Planting a trial](/guide/planting). The variables and factors
come from the ontology ([Describing what you measure](/guide/describing)). Theo Technician, of Field Operations,
records the plots.

![Recording observations](recording.mp4)

## Steps

1. **Submit data starts from the study.** On the study's Datasets, Theo chooses Submit data. {#open}
2. **Locations and units say what was observed.** Long Meadow, then the plots of its 2026 trial area: By type…
   selects all 36 at once. {#units}
3. **The variables and factors are the table's columns.** Plant height (cm) and the Septoria leaf blotch score were
   observed; Nitrogen rate (kg N/ha) is a factor, the treatment each plot received. {#concepts}
4. **Create Data Table lists the plots, and the field sheet is pasted in.** Its three columns go in at once, in the
   sheet's order of plots, with a typo: LM26-207's height went in as 830. {#paste}
5. **📅 gives a column the time of its observations.** The heights were measured on 25 June 2026, and the Septoria
   scored on 16 June. The nitrogen rate held all season, so it takes no time. {#times}
6. **Submit All Datasets stores a dataset of each column.** Theo credits himself, and Field Operations controls the
   datasets, released Private. As each is stored, its column's ⏳ turns ✅, and the study lists it. {#submit}

## Who sees what

> [!SEES]
> Released Private, the datasets are seen by the teams with Read access to Field Operations. A reader who cannot see
> a plot sees its records against a placeholder.

> [!DATA]
> BreedGraph records who submitted each dataset, and when, as it does for every change. The people credited as its
> contributors are person records, shown as in [People and credit](/guide/people).
